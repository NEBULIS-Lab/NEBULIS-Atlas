Frequently Asked Questions

What is Codédex Club?

Codédex Club is a paid membership with a full catalog unlock, 24/7 help from code mentors, unlimited AI, exclusive events such as Office Hours, Resume Review, Interview Prep, and more!


Please review the pricing grid above to see how our membership differs between free and paid tiers.

How can I get in contact with someone from the Codédex team?

Email help@codedex.io to get in touch with our Customer Support team for any questions related to how to use the product, pricing, sponsorship, etc.


For coding-related questions/help, please join our Discord and On-Platform Community (unlockable at 100 XP), and our community moderators & team will assist you there!

How can I contribute to Codédex?

There are many ways to contribute to our platform! We’re always seeking feedback on our product and courses from users.


Please feel free to share your thoughts by emailing us: help@codedex.io.

Can I request a new course, project tutorial, or product feature?

Thanks for your interest in making Codédex great! If you have a course, project tutorial, or product feature that you’d like to see on Codédex, please share it with us.


Please fill out this form to request new content.

What are the prerequisites for learning to code and how can I get started?

None! Most of our users have little to no experience when they sign up. Our courses, such as Learn Python and HTML, are the perfect place to start your coding adventure!

Is Codédex suitable for children?

Yes! If your child can read and write, Codédex is perfect. We recommend getting started with HTML or CSS as these courses are highly visual and engaging.

How do I cancel my subscription?

To cancel your Codédex Club subscription, click on your avatar at the top-right of the page and you’ll see a drop-down. From there, select “Account” → “Billing” and hit the orange “cancel” button.


The last day of your subscription will display underneath the button after clicking cancel.


Please note that once you cancel your subscription, you will lose access to all paid content, even if you have completed it. Should you wish to upgrade your account again, you can do so at any time!
