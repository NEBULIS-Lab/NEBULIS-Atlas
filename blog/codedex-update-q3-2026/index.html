# 🚀 Builds Editor v1.1

We launched Codédex Builds v1.0 back in June, and we've been making it even better, thanks to your feedback! And get this: 183,000+ Builds have already been created by the community, turning ideas into real projects on the web! 🤯

This quarter, we shipped Builds v1.1 with three exciting additions:

  • 🧚 AI-Powered Starter Code
  • 🤝 Builds x Community Integration
  • 🖼️ Preview Page

## 🧚 AI-Powered Starter Code

Putting Lumi to werk.

Ever have an idea for a website but have no clue where to start? We've all been there.

With Builds, you can now generate starter HTML, CSS, JavaScript code in under 30 seconds. Just describe what you want to create, and Lumi will help you get the ball rolling.

There are now three ways to start a Build:

  1. ✨ Describe your idea: Tell Lumi what you want to build. The more details, the better.
  2. 🧩 Choose a template: Right now we have Personal Portfolio, Linktree, and Habit Tracker.
  3. 🗒️ Build from scratch: Start with a blank file and it entirely your own.

Less staring at a blank screen. More building.

Ofc, AI-generated starter code is just that: a starting point. You'll still want to understand all three languages to customize your project and truly make it ✨yours✨.

## 🤝 Builds x Community Integration

Building something awesome is one thing. Sharing it with the world is another. This quarter, we connected Builds more deeply with the Codédex Community.

You can now submit your Builds directly to Monthly Challenges and Project Showcase, making it easier than ever to participate, share your work, and get feedback from fellow learners.

## 🖼️ Preview Pages

Kawaii Piano by @Codebyderkaoui.

So what happens after you submit a Build?

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript Builds now have their own dedicated Preview Pages, complete with a thumbnail image and several ways to explore a project:

  • View in Builds: Open the project in the Builds Editor.
  • Live Site: Experience the finished website.
  • Remix: Make your own version of someone else's creation.
  • Expanded Embed View: Explore the project in a larger preview.

These improvements help turn Builds into more than just a dev environment. They're becoming a way for people to discover ideas, learn from each other, and celebrate what they've created.

Can't wait to see what y'all build next~

# 📛 Introducing 15 New Badges

Been wanting this one for SO long. 😍

Last month, we introduced 15 new Community Badges to to recognize all the different ways Codédex learners show up, participate, and support one another.

Previously, badges were primarily earned by completing courses and course chapters, or participating in special events like hackathons. Now, we’ve added a whole new set of badges to celebrate your involvement in the community.

Let us what know you think here in this Question of the Week.

## New Community Feature: @ Mention

While we're on the topic of Community, here's another long-awaited feature: @ Mentions!

You can now type @username in a community post or comment to find and tag another member. They’ll receive a notification letting them know they’ve been tagged.

Want to bring a friend into a discussion? Ask someone a question? Give credit to a fellow builder? Just @ them! Give it a try the next time you're hanging out in the Community.

# 🏁 On-Platform Checkpoint & Final Projects

This Checkpoint Project is now saved as a Build!

Another big product update for Clubbers: Checkpoint & Final Projects are moving on-platform!

Learners can now complete supported Checkpoint Projects directly using Builds, rather than having to jump between different tools and environments, like Visual Studio Code.

We're starting with the Python course and rolling out support to additional courses as we test, gather feedback, and iron out any issues.

# 🥳 Asiqur ➡️ Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

'S-tier birthday present.'

This one is extra special. 🥹 We're beyond excited to announce that Asiqur Rahman has been promoted to Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at Codédex!

This marks our first ever C-suite promotion, and I couldn't think of anyone more deserving.

Asiqur has been with us for four years, helping build the technology foundation of Codédex. He's grown tremendously as an engineer, manager, and teammate. His recent performance has been nothing short of incredible, with glowing feedback from his peers, direct report, and me.

But what stands out most is that Asiqur thinks like a founder. He takes ownership, cares deeply about the product, and constantly thinks about what's best for the company.

As CTO, Asiqur will take on three major responsibilities:

  • Technology Strategy & Engineering Leadership: Lead our Engineering team, oversee our architecture, and lead the execution of our technical roadmap.
  • Decision-Making & Team Building: Own Eng hiring and management, make key personnel decisions, and approve technical projects and investments.
  • Budget: Manage Eng spending and resources. Yes, he gets a shiny company card. 💳✨

It's been an incredible four years, and I'm SO proud of how far he's come. Please join me in congratulating Asiqur on this well-deserved milestone. 👏

Go show him some love on Codédex (@asiqur), IG (@asiqur.dev), and LinkedIn.
