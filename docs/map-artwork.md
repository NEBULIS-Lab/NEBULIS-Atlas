# Original research archipelago

The map in `public/atlas/research-archipelago.png` was created for NEBULIS Research Adventure with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-04. It is not a captured Codédex scene. A transparent SVG interaction layer and HTML labels connect the illustrated islands to the catalog; the source image contains no UI text.

Final generation brief:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original premium pixel-art game overworld map background for NEBULIS-Lab research learning website, landscape 1536x1024. Create a meticulously hand-crafted 16-bit pixel-art research archipelago, viewed near top-down with slight isometric building depth, crisp pixel clusters and deliberate small dithering; evocative of beautiful indie RPG world maps, not a UI screenshot. Rich deep navy/teal ocean, tiny bright turquoise wave marks, muted jade grass, sandstone shores, brown cliff edges, jewel accent colors, warm cozy building lights. EXACT SEVEN separate islands positioned within safe margins, each approx 18-23% image width: (1) small beginner island lower-left centered at (14%,70%) with cozy yellow-roof explorer cottage, pine grove, campfire and dock; (2) robot learning island upper-left at (23%,31%) with orange-roof mechanical workshop and a recognizable little robot arm; (3) embodied AI island upper-middle at (50%,24%) with bright turquoise robot laboratory and friendly humanoid robot statue, lush vegetation; (4) world models island upper-right at (78%,32%) purple crystal observatory with telescope, midnight indigo roofs; (5) multi-agent island lower-right at (82%,66%) three small green islets linked by wooden bridges with cooperative small robot rovers; (6) distributed AI island lower-middle-right at (58%,76%) pale snowy landscape with blue server towers and satellite dishes; (7) resource harbor island lower-middle-left at (35%,65%) warm ochre archive/library and harbor lighthouse with small boats and pier. Water channels separate all seven; a few dotted nautical paths through water, leave ocean center at (52%,49%) mostly empty for UI overlays. Decorated natural shorelines, dense but carefully organized trees/rocks/grass patches, tiny flowers and little flags, believable layered terrain. No text, NO LETTERS, no labels, no numbers, no frames, no interface, no cards, no watermarks. Individual islands strongly readable as silhouettes. Refined and charming rather than childish. Flat pixel-art rendering, NO smooth vector gradients, NO blurry brushwork, NO photorealism, NO 3D plastic look. Fill canvas with dark ocean, no sky or horizon.

Map labels, interactive hit areas and resource routes are maintained separately in `content/islands.json` and `src/atlas/app.mjs`. The original bitmap is 1536 × 1024. Its layout is used for the world map and cropped region illustrations.
