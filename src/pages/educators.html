Frequently Asked Questions

What’s included in a Codédex education plan?

Get your own Teacher’s Dashboard to manage students, organize your classroom, and track progress all alongside full access to our catalog of coding courses and hands-on projects.

How do you handle student privacy?

Student privacy comes first. Codédex collects minimal student data, requiring only an email address at signup, and takes data privacy and security seriously. We work with schools to understand their specific privacy, security, and compliance requirements to help ensure Codédex meets their needs.

How much does an education plan cost?

Contact our team for a personalized quote tailored to your school’s student count, goals, and classroom needs. We’ll work with you to find the right plan for your students.

Can multiple teachers use it in one school?

Codédex supports multiple teachers across the same school, making it easy to bring coding education to multiple classrooms and help more students learn, build, and create together.

What is the duration of an education plan?

Choose the timeline that works best for your school, with flexible options for a 5-month semester or a 12-month school year.

Does Codédex integrate with Canva or Google Classroom?

Want to bring Codédex into your existing classroom workflow? Contact our team to learn more about available integrations and how Codédex can fit into your teaching setup.
